## 2. Prices

- 986,418 weekly observations, 1988 tickers, 2016-06-03 to 2026-06-26.
- 32 Tel Aviv lines are stamped on Sundays by Bloomberg: moved to the preceding Friday (2-day timing mismatch on returns, no impact on decisions which use no prices).
- `#N/A N/A` (no quote, before IPO / after delisting) are left missing. Gaps of up to 8 weeks are bridged; after the last price a held line earns 0% (cash at the last price).

- Weekly moves above +/-50%: 212 (none: no unit break in EUR prices).

- Smoke test market cap vs price x shares outstanding (last fiscal year): median ratio 1.067, 5th-95th percentile 0.97-1.34; 238 observations outside [0.5, 2] (share buybacks/issuance since the fiscal year, multi-class share counts). Valuation ratios use the market-cap snapshot and company totals, never per-share figures, so they are immune to per-share scale breaks.

## 3. Dividends and the price convention (the main unit risk)

**Finding: PX_LAST is already adjusted for cash dividends.** Event study on all ex-date weeks: regression of the weekly price return on the dividend yield of that week (controlling for the market). With unadjusted prices the slope should be about -1 (the price drops by the dividend); it is about 0:

| yield bucket   |   events |   mean yield |   slope on yield |   t-stat |
|:---------------|---------:|-------------:|-----------------:|---------:|
| 0%-2%          |    32164 |        0.008 |           -0.049 |   -1.209 |
| 2%-5%          |     5781 |        0.030 |           -0.036 |   -0.555 |
| 5%-25%         |      834 |        0.068 |            0.116 |    1.361 |

Corroboration: the price-only benchmark replica returned 12.0%/yr vs 11.7% for MSCI World *Net*, and in the 26/10 Shell test PX_LAST and the Bloomberg total return index grew identically (+14.9% 2023-2025) because the price already embedded the dividends - the test was misread as 'total return fields do not work'. Consequence: gross total return = adjusted price return; DVD_HIST_ALL is used only to size the withholding-tax haircut of the net series. Adding the dividends would have double-counted ~2%/yr. **À confirmer par Noah au terminal (`DPDF`).**

- 44,198 records. Kept as cash: regular, interim, final, special, return of capital, distributions, memorial (Japan). Excluded: stock splits, spin-offs, rights, stock dividends, cancelled/omitted, etc. (1,685 records).
- **Split basis.** Amounts are already on the split-adjusted per-share basis of the prices (NLY 1:4 reverse split 2022: same $0.88 before and after; Tokyo Electron's 2023 3:1 split: fractional pre-split amounts). No split adjustment is applied.

- **Currency.** The amount is in the payment currency, which is not stored in the extract. For sub-unit quotes (GBp, ILs) the amount is either in pence/agorot or in USD: the hypothesis whose median per-payment yield is closest to 1.5% wins. 28 lines are inferred as USD payers (e.g. AZN, BP/, HSBA, GLEN, RIO LN; ICL, TEVA, NICE IT). Limit: a UK payer in GBP or EUR (e.g. DGE, VOD) is converted as USD: error of ~10-25% on its dividend only. **À vérifier par Noah au terminal (`DVD`, colonne currency).**

- **Scale breaks.** 4 tickers have a median single-payment yield > 20% (price series adjusted for an event the dividend history does not follow): their dividends are dropped (8766 JT Equity, FLTR ID Equity, NYKD NO Equity, VIV FP Equity). Single payments yielding > 25% (> 50% for specials) are dropped as errors (48 records).

- **Trailing cap.** Dividends are scaled so that the trailing 12-month yield never exceeds 10%: 384 tickers / 1896 payments affected (yields are measured on dividend-adjusted prices, which sit below actual prices for older dates, plus spin-off adjusted series). This only limits the withholding haircut.

- 359 developed tickers have no usable cash dividend (non-payers, or a Bloomberg error in the batch — SHEL LN is one such case). No withholding haircut is applied to them (their adjusted price still includes dividends): slight favourable bias for payers. **À vérifier par Noah** for the names held in the portfolio (list in `outputs/backtest/holdings_without_dividends.csv`, generated by the backtest).

- Resulting dividend contribution per security (annualised, Jul 2021-Jun 2026): median 0.00%, 95th percentile 0.00%, max 0.00%.

- **Gross vs net.** Adjusted prices embed gross dividends. The benchmark (MSCI World Net) deducts withholding tax. The backtest headline uses a net series built with statutory non-treaty withholding rates by country of risk (`src/returns.py::WHT`, approximations of the MSCI convention, to verify). The gross series is reported as a sensitivity.
