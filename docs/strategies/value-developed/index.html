Systematic Value — Developed Markets

Cheap stocks, measured on several fundamentals and compared only with their sector peers, should outperform over a full cycle. This strategy harvests that premium with transparent rules, controls sector and country bets, and asks one construction question: does an optimiser beat equal weighting on the same ideas?

The signal worked; the portfolio broke even. Cheap stocks beat expensive ones within their sector, and stock selection added +2.3% a year. But the portfolio held no mega-cap growth in 2023-24, sector allocation cost −1.4% and the dollar hedge −0.8%. Net of costs and withholding tax, equal weight matched the benchmark; both optimisers lagged it by more than 2 points a year.
02

Method

Every rule below was fixed before the first backtest ran.

StepRule
UniverseBloomberg World Large & Mid Cap members in 24 developed countries, composition dated at each rebalance; free-float cap above €1bn, 3-month ADV above €5m; one line per issuer
SignalEV/EBIT, P/B, FCF yield and EV/Sales expressed as yields (banks and insurers: P/B and P/E); winsorised at 1/99%; z-scored within GICS sector; equal-weight composite
Accounting lagA fiscal year is used from year-end + 6 months and never before its announcement date
Selection50 names maximising total score in the cheapest sector quintile, within ±10-point sector and country bands versus the benchmark (exact integer programme); a buffer keeps holdings still in the top two quintiles
WeightingEqual weight · minimum variance (Ledoit-Wolf covariance, 250 weeks) · mean-variance; 0.5%-4% per name, long only
RebalancingSemi-annual, ten dates from June 2021 to December 2025; trades at the first Friday after the decision date; 15 bp per trade
CurrencyEUR base; currencies carried by five or more names are hedged with forwards
BenchmarkMSCI World Net Total Return EUR (MSDEWIN)

09

Limits

  • Five years, ten rebalances, one regime. No claim of statistically significant alpha.
  • Bloomberg's World index stands in for MSCI's (MSCI constituents not licensed); a replica built from security returns tracks MSCI World Net with 0.996 correlation.
  • Current GICS sectors applied to history; no market impact in costs; idealised weekly hedge.
  • Negative momentum loading (−0.27): the natural next step is a value + momentum + quality composite.
