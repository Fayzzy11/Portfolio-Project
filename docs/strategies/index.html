Why hold both

Monthly returns in euros, January 2007 – June 2026, rebalanced to the target weights every month.

The two strategies are correlated at 0.64 overall and only 0.42 in the months when world equities fall, because Horus keeps 40% in bonds while the value strategy holds equities and cuts them to 50% on its own signals. Combining them lifts the Sharpe ratio from 0.71 (value alone) and 0.79 (Horus alone) to about 0.85. Two caveats: Horus in euros gained from the rise of the dollar over the period, and the two answer different needs — a 60/40 for a balanced investor, a 100%-equity value strategy for an equity allocation.
