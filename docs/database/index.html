Bloomberg fields

Every field and override used, with the trap each one hides.

FieldMeaningFunctionPitfall
INDX_MWEIGHT_HISTIndex members and weights at a past dateBDSDefault is today's list: always pass END_DATE_OVERRIDE, or the backtest is survivorship-biased.
PX_LASTLast price or index levelBDHFor stocks it came back adjusted for dividends: already a total-return series. Adding dividends double-counts ~2% a year.
PE_RATIOTrailing price-to-earnings of an indexBDHNegative or missing when aggregate earnings are negative: use E/P, not P/E, in the score.
PX_TO_BOOK_RATIOPrice-to-bookBDHStructurally different across sectors: compare each line with its own history.
EQY_DVD_YLD_12MTrailing 12-month dividend yield, %BDHIn percent, not decimal.
DVD_HIST_ALLDividend historyBDSAmount in payment currency, which the extract does not store.
CNTRY_OF_RISKCountry of economic riskBDPCan differ from domicile (Irish-domiciled US groups).
GICS_SECTOR_NAMEGICS sectorBDPCurrent classification only, applied to the whole history.
FILING_STATUS = OROriginally reported figuresoverrideAvoids restated numbers that were not known at the time.
ANNOUNCEMENT_DTResults announcement dateBDHA fiscal year is never used before this date.
CUR_MKT_CAPMarket capitalisationBDHCompany-level total: share classes do not create false discounts.
Currency = EUROverride: convert at Bloomberg ratesoverrideReturns in EUR include currency moves: what a euro investor earns unhedged.
MSDEWIN IndexMSCI World Net TR, EURBDHNet of withholding tax: compare with net, not gross, returns.

Anti-bias checklist

Written before the backtest was interpreted. Rule one: if a result looks good, check survivorship, look-ahead and units first.

#RiskControlStatus
1SurvivorshipUniverse at each date = WORLD composition at that date (10 dated lists), never today's members nor the unionOK
2Survivorship (holding period)A held line that stops trading earns 0% after its last price; no forward-filled gainsOKlimit: a bankruptcy with no final price would be missed (none observed in holdings)
3Look-ahead (accounting)Fiscal year usable from FYE + 6 months and not before announcementOK
4Look-ahead (restatements)Originally reported figures (FILING_STATUS = OR) at extractionOK
5Look-ahead (prices / trading)Decisions use information up to D (cap/float/turnover snapshots at D, returns up to the last Friday ≤ D); trades at the close of the first Friday after DOK
6Look-ahead (classification)GICS sector = current classification applied to the whole historyOpenlimited impact (few re-classifications, mainly 2023 GICS changes for payments/Visa/Mastercard moving to Financials)
7Units: dividends double-countedEvent study proves PX_LAST already embeds dividends: TR = adjusted price returnFixed
8Units: pence / sub-unitsPrices in EUR at source; dividend currency inferred for GBp/ILs (only used for the withholding haircut)OK
9Units: millions / EURFundamentals and market cap both in EUR millions; ratios of company totals onlyOK
10Gross vs net dividendsHeadline uses net-of-withholding returns (MSCI net convention); gross reported as sensitivity (+0.9%/yr for EW)OK
11Index provider mismatchUniverse Bloomberg World DM vs benchmark MSCI WorldDisclosed
12Transaction costs15 bps × two-sided turnover, initial build included; no market impactDisclosed
13FX hedge costCarry from 3-month interbank differentials (covered interest parity), weekly re-struck hedgeDisclosed
14Data snooping in constructionTurnover buffer fixed before seeing the corrected results (target < 60%/yr); a larger buffer did better but was not adoptedOK
15Small sample5 years, 10 rebalances, one regimeDisclosed
