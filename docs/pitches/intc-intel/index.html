In one paragraph

Intel's 2026 recovery came from a shortage: reserved inventory sold in Q1, price rises and a server boom it could only partly supply. The Q4 guide (gross margin 38%, EPS $0.25) shows the peak has passed, yet consensus still models 42.5% and $1.78 of EPS for 2027. We have $1.33 and a $32 target, 43% below the price.

Investment thesis

2026 margins were a windfall, not a new base

Non-GAAP gross margin rose from 41.0% in Q1 to 44.1% in Q3, helped by reserved inventory and price rises, then Intel guided 38% for Q4. We keep 2027 near that run-rate (39.7% non-GAAP) against 42.5% for consensus: 2027E EPS $1.33 vs $1.78.

Supply-capped servers, shrinking PCs

Server CPU demand grows >30% but Intel is short of substrates and Intel 3 output: we model data center +26%. PC units fall ~10% in 2027 and client revenue −9%. Revenue is only 5% below consensus; the EPS gap is margins.

Capex, dilution and minorities absorb the cash

Capex rises 'strongly' in 2027 and free cash flow is negative (−$5.7bn); diluted shares are up 29% since 2023 and ~$1.1bn of profit a year goes to fab partners. At 32.5x Street EPS the stock prices none of this.

Valuation

Blend of DCF, EV/EBITDA and P/E comparables. Price target $32 = DCF $20.96 (30%), EV/EBITDA comps $48.68 (40%), P/E comps $22.31 (30%), rounded; cross-check $1.33 × 24.1x. EV/EBITDA gets most weight because GAAP EPS is distorted by one-offs; the DCF only 30% because 59% of its value is terminal value. The Street's $90.91 target implies 51.2x 2027 EPS.

Catalysts and risks

  • Late Jan 2027

    Q4 2026 results and first 2027 outlook: if consensus moves to $1.33 at its own 51.2x, −$22.93 a share

  • Jan 2027

    IDC/Gartner PC data and CES: confirm a 2027 PC decline (~−10% units)

  • H1 2027

    Data-centre policy after the midterms: each −10% of data center revenue ≈ −$0.19 of EPS

  • Q2-Q3 2027

    First Apple chips on 18A-P: ~$1.2bn of external foundry revenue, too small to change margins

We change the call if
  • Data center revenue above $9bn a quarter at gross margin above 42%
  • Apple volumes confirmed together with a 14A anchor customer and prepayments
  • DRAM contract prices falling early and IDC data showing PCs recovering in H1 2027

Where it fits

Not a holding of the value strategy: Intel was assigned in the SKEMA Equity Research course, where Noah Doose & Victor Endrizzi initiated coverage with a Sell. It is the mirror image of the value pitches: a stock that has fallen 60% and looks cheap on price, but is still expensive on earnings.
