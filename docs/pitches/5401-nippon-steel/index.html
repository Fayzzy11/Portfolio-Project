01

Overview

Nippon Steel entered the portfolio in June 2025, the month it closed the U.S. Steel acquisition, and ranked first of 62 Materials stocks in December 2025. It is a turnaround inside a cyclical industry, so the value signal is only a starting point. This note explains the three engines of the group, the policy context in the US and China, and how to think about book value when returns are depressed.

Nippon Steel trades at about 0.54x book. The market sees a domestic steelmaker hit by Chinese exports. We see a group whose overseas profit is guided from ¥123bn to ¥320bn in one year, led by U.S. Steel behind US tariffs, with permanent financing in place and a ¥24 minimum dividend.

02

The business

Nippon Steel makes flat and long steel products for cars, construction, shipbuilding and energy. In Japan it runs integrated blast-furnace works. Domestic demand is shrinking, so the company has closed capacity (Kashima upstream) and moved customers to contract prices that pass through input costs.

Overseas is the growth engine. U.S. Steel, bought for about $14.9bn in June 2025, makes it a large US producer. The US government holds a golden share that lets it veto plant closures and capacity cuts. AM/NS India, a joint venture with ArcelorMittal, is expanding from about 9 to 15 Mt a year.

Japanese companies report several profit lines. 'Underlying business profit' strips out inventory valuation effects and is the best measure of operating earning power. FY2025 profit to owners was only ¥17bn because of ¥271bn of impairments and losses on exiting AM/NS Calvert and Usiminas.

Domestic steel

Japan's largest integrated steelmaker: ¥528bn of underlying business profit in FY2025, restructured.

U.S. Steel

100% owned since June 2025 (~$14.9bn), under a National Security Agreement with a US golden share.

India and other overseas

AM/NS India JV with ArcelorMittal (capacity 9 to 15 Mt/yr), Thai and ASEAN operations, Ovako, Nippon Steel Slovakia.

Raw materials

Coking coal interests: a partial hedge on input costs.

03

Industry & macro

Global steel is oversupplied. China exported a record of about 119 Mt in 2025, which depresses prices across Asia. That is the main reason the domestic business forecast was cut by ¥90bn in August 2026.

The US is protected. Section 232 tariffs keep imported steel out, so U.S. Steel sells at higher prices. The thesis relies on that protection lasting.

Currency and energy cut both ways. A yen at ¥160 per dollar lifts overseas profits in yen and creates inventory valuation gains (about ¥80bn), while the 2026 Middle East conflict raised raw-material, fuel and logistics costs by about ¥60bn.

06

Investment thesis

Overseas profit is about to more than double

Overseas steel and raw materials made ¥123bn of underlying business profit in FY2025; the FY2026 forecast is ¥320bn. U.S. Steel alone swings from a ¥6bn loss to at least ¥180bn.

The domestic base is restructured

Kashima upstream closure and contract-price reform cut the domestic break-even by 40%. The H2 FY2026 run-rate target was raised to at least ¥900bn of annualised underlying profit.

Financing risk is gone

¥600bn of convertible bonds, a ¥500bn hybrid loan and ¥900bn of JBIC-led loans refinanced the U.S. Steel bridge loan by March 2026. The 2030 plan sets a ¥24 minimum dividend and a D/E target of about 0.7.

Variant perception

Where our reading differs from the obvious one.

What the market says
What the evidence says
“Chinese steel exports will keep crushing spreads.”
They hit the domestic business, which is why we focus on overseas: U.S. Steel sells behind Section 232 tariffs and India grows with local demand.
“U.S. Steel was overpriced and is politically constrained.”
The golden share limits closures, but FY2026 guidance already assumes U.S. Steel at ≥ ¥180bn after the Gary relining.
“The balance sheet is stretched.”
Adjusted D/E is 0.71 and the debt is now long-dated; the CEO calls ~1.0 acceptable during heavy investment.

Valuation

Method: Price to book against the return path. Steelmakers trade on book value adjusted for returns. The 2030 plan targets ROE of about 10%, which would justify roughly 1x book; FY2025 ROE was 0.3%. We apply 0.45x (no recovery), 0.75x (FY2026 guide delivered) and 0.95x (path to the 2030 plan) to book value per share implied by the 0.54x third-party P/B.

We change the call if
  • H2 FY2026 underlying profit annualising well below ¥900bn
  • U.S. Steel profit falling back toward zero, or D/E above ~1.0
  • A cut to the ¥24 minimum dividend or further overseas impairments

The bear case, taken seriously

The bear case: the domestic business is in structural decline, ¥5tn of net debt and convertible dilution cap equity upside, the golden share stops the company from cutting capacity at U.S. Steel, and the profit upgrade relies partly on a weak yen. In that case 0.6x book is a value trap.

12

Interview prep

Questions a portfolio manager is likely to ask, with short answers.

Why is P/B the right metric for a steelmaker?
Earnings swing with the cycle, book value is more stable. The question is the ROE the book can earn: 10% justifies about 1x.
What is the biggest risk?
That U.S. Steel's turnaround depends on tariffs. If protection weakens, the overseas story breaks.
How do the convertibles affect the thesis?
About ¥600bn converting at ~¥733 means roughly 820m new shares. They convert only if the stock rises ~11%, so dilution comes with success.
What will you check at the November results?
Whether H1 underlying profit reaches ¥250bn and the H2 run-rate target of ≥ ¥900bn holds.

Glossary

Underlying business profit
Nippon Steel's operating profit excluding inventory valuation effects.
Golden share
A special share held by a government that gives veto rights over key decisions.
Integrated steelmaker
Makes steel from iron ore in blast furnaces, as opposed to recycling scrap in electric furnaces.
Section 232
US law used to impose tariffs on steel imports on national-security grounds.
