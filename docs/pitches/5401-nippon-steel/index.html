In one paragraph

Nippon Steel trades at about 0.54x book. The market sees a domestic steelmaker hit by Chinese exports. We see a group whose overseas profit is guided from ¥123bn to ¥320bn in one year, led by U.S. Steel behind US tariffs, with permanent financing in place and a ¥24 minimum dividend.

Investment thesis

Overseas profit is about to more than double

Overseas steel and raw materials made ¥123bn of underlying business profit in FY2025; the FY2026 forecast is ¥320bn. U.S. Steel alone swings from a ¥6bn loss to at least ¥180bn.

The domestic base is restructured

Kashima upstream closure and contract-price reform cut the domestic break-even by 40%. The H2 FY2026 run-rate target was raised to at least ¥900bn of annualised underlying profit.

Financing risk is gone

¥600bn of convertible bonds, a ¥500bn hybrid loan and ¥900bn of JBIC-led loans refinanced the U.S. Steel bridge loan by March 2026. The 2030 plan sets a ¥24 minimum dividend and a D/E target of about 0.7.

Valuation

Price to book against the return path. Steelmakers trade on book value adjusted for returns. The 2030 plan targets ROE of about 10%, which would justify roughly 1x book; FY2025 ROE was 0.3%. We apply 0.45x (no recovery), 0.75x (FY2026 guide delivered) and 0.95x (path to the 2030 plan) to book value per share implied by the 0.54x third-party P/B.

Catalysts and risks

  • Early Nov 2026

    H1 FY2026 results: does H1 underlying profit reach ¥250bn?

  • H2 FY2026

    Gary furnace back from relining; Big River 2 ramp-up; Nagoya hot strip mill

  • FY2026

    Asset sales of at least ¥100bn

  • May 2027

    FY2026 results and first FY2027 forecast

  • 2026-2028

    Remaining ~$7bn of the $11bn US investment programme

We change the call if
  • H2 FY2026 underlying profit annualising well below ¥900bn
  • U.S. Steel profit falling back toward zero, or D/E above ~1.0
  • A cut to the ¥24 minimum dividend or further overseas impairments
