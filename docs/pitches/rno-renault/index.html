Renault

The cash pile and the finance arm cover the market value: the car business comes almost free

In one paragraph

Renault trades at about €7.7bn while its car business holds €6.6bn of net cash and its captive bank earns roughly €1.5bn of operating profit a year. The market prices the 2026 margin trough as permanent; we think the reset is done and a product cycle is starting.

Investment thesis

The balance sheet is the floor

Automotive net cash of €6.6bn at June 2026 equals about 86% of the market value. Mobilize Financial Services (the former RCI Banque) adds about €1.5bn of annual operating margin and pays dividends to the car business.

The reset is done

Guidance fell from at least 7% margin (early 2025) to about 5.5% for 2026. That guide was confirmed in February, July and October 2026, and the futuREady plan sets a 5-7% through-cycle band. Expectations are now low and credible.

A product cycle is starting

futuREady plans 36 launches by 2030 on a two-year development cycle. Clio VI, Boreal, Twingo E-Tech and Duster are already on sale, and Q3 2026 revenue beat consensus (+1.4% at constant FX versus −0.3% expected).

Valuation

P/E on 2027 earnings, cross-checked with a sum of the parts. We value Renault on 2027 earnings per share at a single-digit P/E, the range European volume carmakers have traded in through the cycle. Consensus implies about €6.6 of 2026 EPS (price / 4.3x); we use €6.0 in the base case to stay below the street. The sum of the parts is a sanity check, not the target.

Catalysts and risks

  • Nov-Dec 2026

    New Megane E-Tech, Dacia Striker, Sandero hybrid and New Spring launches

  • Year-end 2026

    Inventory back to 525-550k vehicles; free cash flow delivered against the €1.0bn guide

  • Feb 2027

    FY2026 results, dividend proposal, first year of futuREady savings

  • 2026-2027

    Mobilize dividends ramping toward €500m a year; any Nissan stake monetisation

  • Jul 2027

    H1 2027 results: first full read of the cost programme

We change the call if
  • 2026 operating margin below 5%, or automotive FCF well below €1bn
  • Automotive net cash below €5bn without a clear use
  • Another guidance cut at the February 2027 results, or a dividend cut
