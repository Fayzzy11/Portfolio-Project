Carrefour

A grocer priced at a double-digit free-cash-flow yield, with real estate worth more than the equity

In one paragraph

Carrefour fell to €13 after cutting its 2026 guidance on French riots. At that price the market cap (€9.3bn) is below the appraised value of its property (€14.2bn), and even a reduced cash flow gives a double-digit yield. We buy the cash flow and the assets, not a margin recovery story.

Investment thesis

Cash flow at a discount

Net free cash flow was €1.3bn in 2025 (€1.6bn excluding Italy). Even the 'slight decline' now guided for 2026 leaves a free-cash-flow yield above 10% on a €9.3bn market cap.

Assets cover the enterprise value

The property portfolio in France, Spain and Brazil was independently valued at €14.2bn at end-2025, above the market cap plus year-end net debt (about €13.2bn). Sale-and-leasebacks of 35 stores in October 2026 crystallise part of it.

A simpler group

Italy and Romania are sold, Brazil is fully owned, and the three core countries make about 85% of sales and 99% of recurring operating income. Belgium, Poland and Argentina are under review for disposal.

Valuation

Free-cash-flow yield on normalised net free cash flow. A grocer's value is its recurring cash. We divide a normalised net free cash flow by the yield an investor should demand for a low-growth, leveraged retailer (10-12%), then by 707.6m shares. The real-estate appraisal is a floor check, not part of the target.

Catalysts and risks

  • Nov-Dec 2026

    Evolution of the French unrest; insurance and state compensation

  • Feb 2027

    FY2026 results: dividend under the new payout policy; real-estate revaluation

  • Q1 2027

    Decisions on Belgium, Poland and Argentina

  • Jul 2027

    H1 2027: first test of the 3.2% margin path to 2028

  • 2027

    Brazil rate cuts helping Atacadão volumes

We change the call if
  • 2027 ROI margin not above 2025's 2.6%
  • Cumulative 2026-28 net FCF tracking well below €5bn
  • S&P outlook turning negative, or disposals that dilute EPS without deleveraging
