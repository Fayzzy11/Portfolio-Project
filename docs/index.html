  1. Keep a permanent 60/40

    The split between equities and bonds never moves. Only the funds inside it change.

    Equities 60%
    Bonds 40%

  2. Each January, pick the funds that carry the factors

    Twelve equity ETFs are scored on size, growth and momentum over three years: the best two get 30% each. The two bond ETFs with the best Sharpe ratio get 20% each.

    IWM 30%XLK 30%
    LQD 20%RXR 20%
    Portfolio for 2026: US small caps, US technology, US investment-grade corporates, German government bonds

  3. Hold four funds for twelve months

    No trading during the year. The factors stay the same; the funds that carry them rotate.
