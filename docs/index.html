§ 04

House rules

The constraints every result on this site respects.

Parameters before results

Universe, signal, constraints and costs are written down before any backtest runs. Every variant tested is shown, not only the best one.

No survivorship, no look-ahead

Index membership is taken as it was at each date. Accounts are used six months after year-end and never before publication.

Too good means check first

A result that beats the benchmark by a steady margin is treated as a data error until proven otherwise. One such check found a 2%-a-year bug.

Costs and taxes included

15 bp per trade, dividend withholding tax as in the MSCI net convention, and the carry cost of currency hedges.
