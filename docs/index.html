Equity research & portfolio construction

I am Noah Doose, MSc Financial Markets & Investments student at SKEMA Business School (Paris) and CFA Level II candidate. This site is my research base: systematic strategies built and backtested end to end, research notes on what I learned along the way, and fundamental stock pitches.

Everything here is designed to be checked: dated universes (no survivorship), lagged accounting data (no look-ahead), every decision logged with its alternatives, and honest results — including when a strategy does not beat its benchmark.
